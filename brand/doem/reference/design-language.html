What the new mark should fit

Matches the app

  • Simple geometry with rounded ends, the same family as the 1.5 px icons.
  • Ink and white, with at most one touch of the indigo accent.
  • Calm, not loud. The app is a quiet desk, not a productivity sprint.
  • Holds up as one flat colour at 16 px for the menu bar.

Ideas the name gives you

  • "Do" and "diem": a day, a mark ticked off, a sun or circle completed.
  • One item picked from a set, as in Up next.
  • The lowercase d or the double o-shape in the wordmark.

Avoid

  • Clocks and metronomes. Those belonged to the old name.
  • Gradients inside the mark, or thin hairlines that vanish at small sizes.
  • Latin flourishes, laurels or serif crests. The Latin is in the name, not the look.
