Your day, at a glance.

Doem is a Mac app for tasks, planning and time tracking, with your data kept on your own Mac.

Download for Mac ↓

Apple Silicon, macOS 12 or later · Version 1.0.0

What's inside

The day, the month and the hours, in one window.

Today

Start with what's in front of you.

Today lists what is due now, with times and subtasks. Quick add captures a thought without leaving the list, and a global shortcut opens a small capture window from any app.

  • Search and a command palette
  • Reminders and a running timer in the status bar
  • A wrap-up at the end of the day

Calendar

See the month without leaving the app.

A month grid with a panel for the selected day. Subscribe to your tasks as a calendar feed, and show iCloud events beside them.

Tracking

Know where the hours went.

Track time against a task or a category, fill in the gaps you missed, and read the day back with rollups for the week.

Projects

Move work across stages.

Each project has a board with stages you define. Drag a card forward when the work moves; it is the same task everywhere else in the app.

Native where it counts

Hands stay on the keyboard.

A real Mac menu bar with the shortcuts you would expect.

  • Search⌘F
  • Inbox⌘0
  • Today⌘1
  • Calendar⌘2
  • Tracking⌘3
  • Wrap up the day⌘⇧E

Privacy

Local by design.

A local database

Your tasks live in local PGlite data on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Doem. Nothing is uploaded.

No account

There is nothing to sign up for and no server to sign in to. This page has no tracking scripts.

Backups are yours

Time Machine covers it. To copy the folder by hand, quit Doem first.

First launch

The app is not signed yet.

Doem is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS may refuse to open it the first time. Open the DMG, drag Doem to Applications, then:

  1. Right-click Doem in Applications and choose Open.
  2. Confirm in the dialog. macOS remembers the choice afterwards.

If macOS still blocks it, allow it under System Settings, Privacy & Security.
